About

I'm a PhD-level, licensed clinical social worker and Associate Professor of Social Work — and also a woman navigating menopause while running a full caseload, a professorship, and a public-facing career of my own. I know firsthand what it's like to carry a high-stakes job while your hormones, sleep, and focus are shifting underneath you, and still be expected to perform at the same level every day.

That's not a contradiction I work around. It's exactly why I do this work.

I write, teach, and practice around one idea: mental health belongs in your daily routine, right alongside training, sleep, and the people you love — not something you reach for only when things fall apart. Because I live the same pressure, my perspective isn't theoretical. It's the same routine-based mental training I use to keep showing up for my own high-stakes days.
